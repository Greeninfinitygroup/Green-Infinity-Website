About Green Infinity

Research driven by sustainable thinking.

Green Infinity is a research group under the Institute of Materials Research and Engineering (IMRE), working at the intersection of sustainable materials, advanced processing and biomedical engineering.

Our research seeks to transform renewable and sustainable resources into functional materials with meaningful scientific and technological applications.

By connecting materials science, processing, engineering and biomedical applications, we aim to develop solutions that contribute to a more sustainable future.

01

Sustainable Materials

Developing materials from renewable and sustainable resources while exploring pathways toward higher-value applications.

02

Advanced Processing

Combining materials science with advanced processing and manufacturing approaches to create functional material platforms.

03

Translational Research

Connecting fundamental materials research with applications in biomedical engineering and beyond.
