ABOUT

Researching materials for a more sustainable future.

Green Infinity is a research group under A*STAR focused on developing innovative materials and technologies that address sustainability and biomedical challenges.

Our research brings together materials science, sustainable processing, biomaterials and advanced manufacturing to transform renewable resources into functional solutions.

01

Sustainability

Developing materials and processes that make better use of renewable resources and reduce environmental impact.

02

Scientific Excellence

Combining fundamental research with rigorous materials characterization and advanced processing.

03

Translation

Moving promising research from laboratory concepts toward practical applications.

RESEARCH

Our Research

Our research focuses on renewable resources, sustainable materials and biomedical applications, connecting fundamental science with real-world needs.

01

SUSTAINABLE MATERIALS

Lignin-based Functional Materials

Developing functional materials from lignin, a renewable and underutilized component of biomass, with a focus on unlocking new applications for sustainable materials.

Learn more →
02

ADVANCED PROCESSING

Lignocellulose Pretreatment & 3D Printing

Investigating lignocellulosic biomass pretreatment and advanced manufacturing approaches, including 3D printing, to create sustainable material platforms.

Learn more →
03

BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERING

Biomedical Scaffolds for Tissue Engineering

Designing advanced scaffold materials for tissue engineering and biomedical applications, with emphasis on material functionality and biological performance.

Learn more →

OUR APPROACH

From renewable resources to functional materials.

Green Infinity combines sustainable resource utilization, materials chemistry, characterization and advanced processing to develop materials with meaningful applications.

By connecting different areas of materials science, we seek to create new pathways for sustainable manufacturing and biomedical innovation.
